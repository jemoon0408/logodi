Overview Programs

Introduction

LOGODI's international program is a cornerstone of its broader mandate to contribute toward better governance in the world. The program is designed to share Korea's experiences in the local administrative system and regional development with foreign officials from partner countries and support sustainable development strategies of their countries. LOGODI further provides follow-up management through on-site training by invitation of a host government or institution.

Programs in Korea

Invitational Training Programs are funded by official development assistance (ODA) from the Korean Government and designed and operated by LOGODI. Upon an official request from a country, further sponsorship may be provided by the Korea International Cooperation Agency (KOICA) for a customized program to respond to the training needs of its officials.

LOGODI operates On-site Training programs overseas at the invitation of a host government or institution, often via alumni as its representatives.
