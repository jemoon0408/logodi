International Organizations

EROPA

EROPA (Eastern Regional Organization for Public Administration) information
Summary Eastern Regional Organization for Public Administration (EROPA)
Content EROPA works toward social and economic advancement in countries across the Asia-Pacific, by promoting the development of public administration in the region. It is accredited by the UN Public Administration Network (UNPAN) and produces regional conferences, seminars, training programs, studies, surveys, and publications. It was founded in 1960, and is headquartered in Manila with regional centers in the Republic of Korea (Development Management Center), Japan (Local Government Center), and New Delhi (Training Center).

LOGODI has been a member of EROPA since 2006.

IASIA

IASIA (International Association of School and Institutes of Administration) information
Summary International Association of School and Institutes of Administration (IASIA)
Content IASIA is an global association whose mission is to strenghten administrative capacity building around the world, to advance excellence in public administration education and training, and to conduct and discuss and disseminate cutting edge scholarly research and smart practices on governance and administration.

UCLG - ASPAC

UCLG - ASPAC (United Cities and Local Governments Asia Pacific) information
Summary United Cities and Local Governments Asia Pacific (UCLG-ASPAC)
Content United Cities and Local Governments (UCLG) represents over 240,000 local and regional governments from 175 countries in their interests toward fair and sustainable societies based on local democracy, self-government, and decentralization. It was established with the merge of the International Union of Local Authorities (IULA) and United Towns Organization (UTO) in 2004, and is headquartered in Barcelona with regional sections in Africa, Asia-Pacific, Eurasia, Europe, Latin America, Middle East/West Asia, and North America, along with a metropolitan section and a forum of regions.

LOGODI has been a member of UCLG preceding its current structure since 1981.
