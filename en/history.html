History

  1. 2021

    2021
    Opened the Assessment and Development Center & Local Council Training Center

  2. 2013

    2013
    Relocated to Jeonbuk Innovative City, in following with the government's initiative for "balanced regional development."

  1. 2005

    2005
    Reorganized as the Local Government Officials Development Institute (LOGODI).

  2. 2000

    2000
    Initiated the International Training Program (ODA)

  3. 1999

    1999
    Merged with training institutions under six ministries and administrations to form the National Institute of Professional Administration (NIPA).

  4. 1996

    1996
    Korea - Japan Exchange Program begun

  1. 1978

    1978
    Relocated to Suwon, in accordance with a government policy to reduce congestion in the capital.

  1. 1965

    1965
    Established under the Ministry of Home Affairs as the Local Administration Training Institute (LATI) in Seoul.
